CompatibilityWhich boards does it support?

The jig only carries six signals: EN, 3.3 V VDD, TX, RX, GND and one boot pin, which is pulled low by the flasher's auto-program circuit. So it supports every chip whose ROM bootloader is entered by holding one pin low during reset over UART. That covers the whole ESP family, plus UPDI AVRs through a small wiring variant.

Important: the pogo pins press on pads on your own PCB, not on a module's castellated edge. An ESP-12F, ESP32-S3-MINI-1 or ESP32-C6-MINI-1 is supported when the board you design around it has the 6 PROG pads wired as below. For a loose module with no carrier board, use the flasher's H1 wires or the ESP-01 socket.

Boot pins come from Espressif's esptool "Boot Mode Selection" docs. UART0 pins come from ESP-IDF soc/<chip>/include/soc/uart_pins.h. Both checked 2026-11-13.

InsideHow it works

J1 and the pogo tips carry the same 6 signals in the same order, so the cable from the flasher is straight: pin 1 to pin 1.

📍

3 pogo strips = 2 × 3 grid

CHIN-BAN PG2541-02-002P strips have 2 pins at 2.54 mm and a 2.50 mm wide housing. Three stacked rows give the PROG grid with about 0.04 mm to spare.

🔁

Auto-program through the tip

Pad 1 (EN) and pad 6 (boot pin) are driven by the flasher's Q1/Q2, so esptool resets the chip into its bootloader by itself.

🔋

Current-limited 3.3 V

VDD is the flasher's switched +3V3_T (≈0.6 A limit). Hold POWER on the flasher to power-cycle the target. The jig's green LED shows VDD at the tip.

🎯

Guide pins

Optional Ø1.0 mm dowels drop into the PROG standard's locating holes first, so the pogo tips land square on the pads.

Bring-upAssembly checklist

Tick as you go.

FlashingUse

Set the flasher

SW3 = 3V3, SW4 = UART. For UPDI targets, SW4 = UPDI.

Connect

6-way 2.54 mm cable from the flasher's J4 to the jig's J1, pin 1 to pin 1. J4 isn't keyed, so match the pin-1 marks.

Press

Guide pins into the locating holes, pogo tips onto the pads (pad 1 = pin-1 dot). The green LED lights.

Flash

esptool.py --port COM7 write_flash 0x0 firmware.bin. Auto-program handles reset and boot.
