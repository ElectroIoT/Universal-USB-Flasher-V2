External wiring (what you connect by hand)

ConnectionHow
USB-C (USB1)To the PC. Two COM ports appear: UART1 = H1 / J3 / J4 / J2, UART2 = J5.
Main header H1Pin 1 3V3, 2 5V (both switched, current-limited), 3 GND, 4 TX -> target RX, 5 RX/UPDI <- target TX, 6 DTR, 7 RTS, 8 RST (Arduino), 9 EN, 10 IO0, 11 IO15.
ESP-Prog cable (J3)1.27 mm 2x3 cable to boards with Espressif's PROG header. Same order as ESP-Prog: EN, VDD, TXD, GND, RXD, IO0.
Pogo jig / 2.54 cable (J4)Same pinout as J3 at 2.54 mm; the PROG pogo jig plugs in here.
ESP-01 (J2)Plug the module in, antenna away from the header. SW3 must be on 3V3.
UART2 (J5)1 GND, 2 TX2 -> device RX, 3 RX2 <- device TX, 4 DTR2 (raw).
SW3 / SW4SW3 = logic level 3V3 or 5V (5V lights LED5). SW4 = UART or UPDI (TX linked to RX through R15).
SW5 POWERHold to switch both target rails off (power-cycle).

Checks found in your schematic:
  1. Sheet 1: no +5V label on the D1 cathode / C5 / U1 pin 3 VIN node. Add +5V there, otherwise U5, LED6, U6/U7 and SW3 pin 3 get no 5 V.
  2. Sheet 1: "V IO" still has a space - rename to VIO (Q3 emitter is not joined to VIO on sheets 4 and 7).
  3. Sheet 2: U4 pin 6 OUT has no label - add +3V3_T (C8, H1, J2-J4 and R9-R13 have no 3.3 V without it).
  4. Sheets 1-2: "+3v3" (small v) but sheet 7 SW3 uses +3V3 - rename the sheet 1-2 labels to +3V3.
  5. Sheet 5: SJ2 pin 1 is labelled DTR_N - it must be RTS_N (both jumpers on DTR_N breaks auto-program).
  6. Sheet 7: SW3 pin 4 and SW4 pin 4 have No-Connect flags - remove the flags, connect pin 4 to GND (mounting tab).
  7. Sheet 2: the red OVERLOAD LED next to R21 has no designator - name it LED6.

Sheet 1: Power (F1, D1, LDO, bulk caps, 5V-IO LED (Q3))

USB fuse F1, back-feed diode D1, bulk capacitors

VBUS -> F1 -> VBUS_F (C1 10 uF to GND). D1 anode (triangle) on VBUS_F, cathode (bar) on +5V. C5 10 uF from +5V to GND - never across D1.

3.3 V regulator U1 (SGM2212)

Pin 3 VIN = +5V, pin 1 GND, pins 2 and 4 VOUT = +3V3. C6 22 uF and C7 100 nF on +3V3.

5V-IO warning LED (Q3, R16, R17, LED5)

PNP Q3: emitter (arrow, bottom) on VIO, base via R16 4.7k to +3V3, collector via R17 1k to LED5 anode; LED5 cathode (bar) to GND. Net name VIO - no space.

ESP-01 bulk capacitor C8

C8 22 uF from +3V3_T to GND; on the PCB next to J2 VCC.

Sheet 2: Target Power (TPS2553 switches, POWER-CYCLE, PWR + OVERLOAD LEDs)

3.3 V target switch U4 + PWR LED

U4: 1 IN = +3V3 (C11), 2 GND, 3 EN = TPWR_EN, 4 /FAULT = FAULT_N, 5 ILIM -> R18 43k -> GND, 6 OUT = +3V3_T (C12). PWR LED: +3V3_T -> R3 1k -> LED1 anode, cathode (bar) -> GND.

5 V target switch U5

U5: 1 IN = +5V (C13), 2 GND, 3 EN = TPWR_EN, 4 /FAULT = FAULT_N, 5 ILIM -> R19 51k -> GND, 6 OUT = +5V_T (C14).

Enable line + POWER-CYCLE button

TPWR_EN: R20 100k to +3V3, SW5 to GND. Put a TPWR_EN label on U4 pin 3 AND U5 pin 3.

OVERLOAD LED

+5V -> R21 1k -> LED6 anode; LED6 cathode (bar) -> FAULT_N. FAULT_N label on U4 pin 4 AND U5 pin 4.
