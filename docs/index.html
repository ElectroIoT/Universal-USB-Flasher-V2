ReviewWhat V2 fixes from V1

These came from reviewing the V1 CH340C board (schematic, BOM and PCB in the GitHub repo).

1
Q2 (IO0 transistor) drawn with collector on DTR and emitter on IO0, which runs it backwards at very low gain.
Fixed: C = IO0, B = RTS# via 10k, E = DTR# (NodeMCU circuit).
2
"SJ" jumpers are really 2-pin headers, so auto-program is off until you fit jumper caps.
Fixed: real solder jumpers, bridged by default.
3
Pin labelled "RST" was raw RTS. No proper Arduino reset.
Fixed: RST pin = DTR# through 100 nF, plus a 10k pull-up.
4
CH340C at 3.3 V: a 5 V Arduino TX drives 5 V into a 3.3 V input.
Fixed: CH342F VIO pin + slide switch: true 3.3 V or 5 V logic, with a 5V-IO warning LED.
5
No fuse: a short on the 5V pin goes straight back to the PC.
Fixed: 500 mA PTC on VBUS, plus current-limited switches on the target 3V3 and 5V outputs.
6
"PWD" typo, a 13 mA power LED, and README values that don't match the BOM.
Fixed: PWR label, 1.3 mA LED, docs match the BOM.

What it doesFeatures

Each feature is a small block on the schematic. Section 4 shows every one wire by wire.

⚡

3.3 V / 5 V logic + warning LED

SW3 sets CH342F VIO for both UARTs. A red 5V IO LED lights whenever the lines swing at 5 V, so you notice before plugging in an ESP.

🔀

Dual UART (CH342F)

Two COM ports. UART1 flashes the target with auto-program; UART2 on J5 watches a second device's log at up to 3 Mbaud.

🔁

ESP auto-program

Two MMBT3904 transistors drive EN and IO0 from DTR/RTS. esptool, Arduino IDE and PlatformIO flash with no button pressing.

🔌

ESP-Prog compatible port

J3 is Espressif's 2×3 1.27 mm PROG box header (EN · VDD · TXD · GND · RXD · IO0). J4 has the same pinout at 2.54 mm for 2.54 cables or a pogo jig.

🔋

Current-limited target power

Two TPS2553 switches (≈0.61 A on 3V3, ≈0.51 A on 5V) cut a short in microseconds. Hold POWER-CYCLE to switch the target off; OVERLOAD lights on a trip.

🛡️

ESD on every line

USBLC6-2SC6 on USB, and two USBLC6-4SC6 on TX, RX, DTR, RTS, EN, IO0, TX2 and RX2. Plus a 500 mA PTC and a Schottky back-feed diode.

📡

ESP-01 socket

Plug an ESP-01/01S into J2 and flash. GPIO2, RST and CH_PD pull-ups are on the board.

🧩

Bare ESP-12F / WROOM

EN and IO0 pull-ups plus an IO15 pin with a 10k pull-down. A bare module or pogo jig needs only the header wires.

♾️

Arduino auto-reset

RST pin = DTR# via 100 nF. Works with a bare ATmega328P, Pro Mini, LGT8F328P and similar.

🔧

UPDI mode

SW4 links TX to RX through 4.7k, so RX becomes a SerialUPDI pin for ATtiny 0/1/2-series and AVR-Dx.

📐

PROG pogo-pad standard

Future ElectroIoT boards carry 6 bare pads in ESP-Prog order at 2.54 mm. A pogo jig on J4 flashes them with no header fitted.

💡

6 status LEDs

Target PWR, TX, RX, UART2 RX, 5V IO and OVERLOAD. The data LEDs run from VIO, so they work at either logic level.

4.1 Power path

The CH342F core runs from VBUS_F (5 V). Its I/O runs from VIO. The 5 V logic level is the +5V rail after D1 (~4.6–4.7 V), which is inside the VIO 1.8–5 V range.

4.2 CH342F (U2, QFN-24)

Pin 6 V3 gets only 100 nF (VDD5 is 5 V). DTR0# (23) and DTR1# (15): no pull-down, because a 3–8k pull-down at power-up switches them to TNOW (RS485 mode). Solder the exposed pad (pin 0) to GND with vias. Unused modem pins get No-Connect flags.

4.4 UPDI mode + Arduino reset

With SW4 on UART the board is a normal serial adapter. With SW4 on UPDI, connect RX/UPDI, GND and VCC, then pick "SerialUPDI" in megaTinyCore/DxCore.

4.5 ESP-01 socket (J2)

Component side up, antenna pointing away from the header. Check this against your module: some clones differ. Set SW3 to 3V3 before plugging in an ESP-01.

4.7 5V-IO warning LED (Q3)

No DPDT switch needed. The PNP compares VIO against the 3.3 V rail, so the red LED is on only in 5 V mode (≈2 mA). Silk next to it: 5V IO!

4.8 PROG port (J3 1.27 mm · J4 2.54 mm) + UART2 (J5)

Pin order follows Espressif's ESP-Prog Program interface: ESP_TXD is the target's TX, so it goes to the flasher RXD. VDD is fixed to +3V3_T (switched, current-limited).

PCBBoard design and footprint placement

Suggested 80 × 40 mm, 2-layer, 1.6 mm board. Power flows left to right, the CH342F sits in the middle, the target switches flank it, and the target I/O is on the right. The ESP-01 hangs off the bottom edge so it covers no LEDs or buttons. Coordinates are a starting point for EasyEDA (origin top-left, mm). Move parts as routing needs.

USB + ESD

U3 within 3 mm of USB1. Route D+/D− short and side by side over solid GND, with no vias. At USB full-speed, controlled impedance isn't critical over this length.

Decoupling

C2 (VDD5), C3 (V3) and C4 (VIO) within 2 mm of U2 pins 7, 6 and 5. QFN pad to GND with 4 vias. C6/C7 right at U1 VOUT.

LDO heat

U1 SOT-223 tab on at least 1 cm² of top copper, with 4–6 vias to the bottom GND pour. ESP32 Wi-Fi peaks reach ~500 mA.

Controls on the edge

SW5 and SW1–SW4 along the top edge with big silk labels: POWER RESET BOOT IO 3V3|5V UART|UPDI.

ESP-01 overhang

J2 on the bottom edge with a dashed module outline in silk. No copper needed under the module footprint beyond the edge.

ESD placement

U6 and U7 sit between the header pins and everything else, with short GND vias. Route the signal through the ESD pad on its way to the header.

Test pads

VBUS_F, +5V, +3V3, +3V3_T, +5V_T, VIO, GND, TXD, RXD, TX2, RX2, DTR#, RTS# as 1.5 mm pads on the bottom side.

Bring-upFirst-power test plan

Nothing here has been built yet. Do these steps in order on the first board.

No shorts

Before fitting U1/U2, check there's no short between VBUS, +5V, +3V3, +3V3_T, +5V_T and GND.

Rails

+5V ≈ 4.6–4.7 V, +3V3 = 3.30 V. +3V3_T/+5V_T follow them, VIO follows SW3, PWR LED on.

Enumerate

Two COM ports appear (CH342 dual serial). UART1 = H1/J3/J4, UART2 = J5.

Loopback

Bridge TX↔RX and TX2↔RX2. Type at 115200, then 2 Mbaud. TX, RX and RX2 LEDs blink.

5V-IO LED

SW3 = 3V3: LED5 off. SW3 = 5V: LED5 on and TX idles at ≈4.6 V.

Power-cycle

Hold SW5. +3V3_T and +5V_T drop to 0 V and the PWR LED goes off. Release: they come back.

Overload

Load +3V3_T with ≈0.8 A (or 3.3 Ω). The current holds at ≈0.6 A and OVERLOAD lights. Repeat on +5V_T (≈0.5 A).

Auto-program

esptool.py chip_id through H1, then through J3 with an ESP-Prog cable: EN pulses low, IO0 is held low as EN rises.

Real targets

ESP32 DevKit, bare ESP-12F, ESP-01, Pro Mini 5 V via RST, ATtiny1614 via UPDI.
