Review of SCH_Schematic1_2026-11-13.pdfWhat your schematic needs

All 64 real parts are placed on 8 pages with the correct values and LCSC parts. Before wiring, fix these four things:

1 · Rename U8 → U2

The CH342F is U8 in your file. Every document, the BOM and the placement use U2: double-click the designator and change it.

2 · Add SJ1 and SJ2 (page 5)

Two 2-pin headers C492401 (PZ254V-11-02P). SJ1: pin 1 DTR_N, pin 2 DTR_AP. SJ2: pin 1 RTS_N, pin 2 RTS_AP. Fit jumper caps (C5305) = auto-program ON.

3 · SW3/SW4 pin 4 → GND

Your MSK12C02 symbol has a 4th pin (metal mounting tab). Tie it to GND so it is not floating.

4 · CH342F pad = pin 25

Your symbol numbers the exposed pad EP 25. Connect it to GND (this page already uses 25).

MethodHow to wire it fast in EasyEDA

🏷️

Net label on each pin

Draw a short wire from the pin, then Place → Net Label and type the name exactly as shown (e.g. TXD). Same name = connected, even on another page.

⏚

Power symbols

GND, +3V3 and +5V use Place → Net Port / Power flag with that exact name. Every other rail (VBUS_F, VIO, +3V3_T…) is a normal net label.

✕

No-Connect flags

Pins shown with a red ✕ get Place → No Connect Flag. 13 in total.

✅

Then verify

Run ERC, export the netlist (Design → Netlist) and compare with hardware/netlist.csv: 43 nets.
